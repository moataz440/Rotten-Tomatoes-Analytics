Summary

Key Findings

The most important takeaways from the analysis.

📊

Moderate critic–audience agreement

Pearson r ≈ 0.67. Critics and audiences broadly agree, but diverge significantly in certain genres and for extreme scores.

🎯

Certified Fresh films earn audience trust

Certified Fresh movies average ~20+ audience-rating points higher than Rotten films, showing that critic consensus is a reliable quality signal.

🎬

Genre drives the critic–audience gap

Horror and Action audiences rate far more generously than critics. Documentary and Drama see the opposite — critics are the bigger fans.

⏱️

Rotten films stream sooner

The median streaming window for Rotten films is noticeably shorter than for Fresh or Certified Fresh films, suggesting studios fast-track underperformers to VOD.

🏆

Studio quality is consistent

The top 15 studios (min 30 films) all average above 60% on the Tomatometer, with prestige and arthouse labels leading by a wide margin.

📅

The critic–audience gap is widening

Since the early 2000s, average critic scores have trended upward while audience scores have stayed flatter — the gap is a recent and growing phenomenon.
